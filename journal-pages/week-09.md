### Notebook LM Project Statement

I am exploring the pressing public issue of stray cat overpopulation in Aotearoa New Zealand, where introduced predators threaten native bird and lizard species. My work presents a data-driven visualisation designed as a "stray cat tracker". The installation is configured with one side representing a map of New Zealand and the other displaying images of cats that appear in need of desexing, paired with their specific locations. 

This project utilises spatial data derived from Auckland SPCA spreadsheets, which record thousands of cat pickups and admissions. These records have been geocoded and matched with census databases to identify geographic "hot spots" and explore how socioeconomic conditions relate to stray cat density. To envision a live system, the work also incorporates the potential for real-time data collected through images and timestamps, though it may employ simulated data or random images from a cat API where existing sources are insufficient. 

My proposed future scenario involves a tracker that informs community members about the presence of stray cats in their area, including information on who is feeding them and whether they are hunting native wildlife. This system acts as an analyser to determine if a cat requires veterinary treatment or desexing to minimise unfavourable impacts on the environment. 

In developing this work, I am engaging critically with the "datafication of everyday life" and the concept of data physicalisation. I have intentionally avoided AI usage to prioritise human input and curation, believing that a reliance on automated tasks can erode critical thinking skills. By portraying a normalised part of our urban landscape through data, I aim to reveal the deeper implications of cat overpopulation. My hope is that the audience will realise the complexity of this issue and change their perspective on the impacts of stray cats. Ultimately, I want to encourage ways to value feline companionship without compromising the safety of our native species.
