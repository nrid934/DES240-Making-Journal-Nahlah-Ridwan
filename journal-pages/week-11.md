## Making of the final artefact

Throughout the week I began making and finalising my data visualisation project. I first started by finalising that I would use acrylic for the main material of the physical outcome, I found that timber could potentially draw more attention on itself than needed, and in this case I wanted to prioritise the data being shown and noticable.

As I did not pay for a subscription to the Adobe suite, I had to work on the Illustrator file on the univerisity's design computers. Using my concept sketch and the Science Direct data image as references, I made most of the file. Please note that some images used may have captions underneath them to provide more context.

Next, i wanted to use white acrylic for the shape of the cat. At this point I realised I wanted to change the initial shape of the feline, to better fit my altered future scenario; inspiring others to desex their cats. As a way to draw a connection between the overpopulation data and desexing cats, I chose to show the back view of a cat to attempt provoking thought in the viewer.

To showcase the cat overpopulation numbers, I had the idea of using red pom poms. With pom poms there was the potential to have a sensory interaction with the piece and perhaps a connection to the feeling of cats. However, given that it was already week 11, I could only purchase pom poms in a physical store.

https://www.spotlightstores.com/en-nz/p/crafters-choice-assorted-pom-poms/BP80344576-red 
I looked into these pom poms from Spotlight. The sizings were different and I was unable to think of a way to use all sizes to minimise waste. I also found that the sizes of the pom poms were bigger than I intially would have liked (around 3-4mm size). In the end, I chose to not use the pom poms and instead, laser cut the data points.
