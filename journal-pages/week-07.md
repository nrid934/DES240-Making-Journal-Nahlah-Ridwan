My initial idea was to create a project which showcased the process in which a cat would go through if it were experiencing the stages of TNR (trap, neuter, return). However this was found to be impractical as it did not fit a dataset that I was looking at.

### Making sprint
For the making sprint, I aimed to sketch conceptual ideas for my final outcome.

![alt text](<../assets/week-07/Screenshot 2026-06-05 224537.png>)
Inspired by a New Zealand souvenir, I wanted to explore creating a rotating piece which allowed the observer to see different sides of the same issue.

![alt text](<../assets/week-07/Screenshot 2026-06-05 224545.png>)
This display I wanted to use s a way to familiarise more people into the concepts of overpopulating cats and how we could attempt mitigating it.

### Peer activity
What if the design was virtual instead?

If the design was virtual instead, I would maybe have made it into a game-like format where a cat goes through the TNR/TAR process and the user completes the tasks that take place.

What if you used a hill instead of a circle? 

There’s a hill where a figure can go to the top on its own, could be like a cat getting better
I really liked this idea, the subtle symbolism to show that the cat gets better is quite nice as it would require less text to show that this process helps the cats long term.

What if the design was at a different scale?

I can’t see the design getting any smaller, if it was big I could imagine it being the scale of people, making people seem as though they are the cats going through the process. However this would be very impractical as there would not be enough time for executing something at this scale.
