## Documentation 

Progress Reports:
During class we had formed groups and completed progress reports. Each report lasted about 5 minutes and we formed our own questions in which we wanted to recieve feedback about from peers.



[Link to Slideshow](<../assets/week-08/DES240 - 5 Minute Presentation.pdf>)

Feedback recieved from peers:
- You could show what is fun about cats
- You could use a cat meme dataset
- Maybe aim to go for a dataset that triggers something within you
- You could do cat spatial population but in a fun way
- Something that when you look at it, it teaches you something you didn't know before
- Could lean into the humourous side
- A physical model could be made to teach something, whilst also stretching your skills as a designer
- Designing WITH data 

My own reflection:
Through this class time and activity, I found that I was quite behind compared to my peers. I had yet to finalise a dataset that I wanted to use whereas they had. My peers already began thinking of ideas to display the data whereas I only had a rough idea of how I wanted to make a physical prototype.
